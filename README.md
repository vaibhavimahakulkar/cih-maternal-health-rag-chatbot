# AI-Powered Maternal Health Risk Assessment Chatbot

Built during **Central India Hackathon – Chapter 3 (CIH 3.0)**  
Aligned with **UN SDG 3 – Good Health & Well-Being**

---

## 🧩 Problem Statement
Early detection of pregnancy-related health risks remains a challenge, especially in rural and resource-limited settings. Delays in identifying high-risk conditions can lead to serious complications for both mother and child.

---

## 🚀 Proposed Solution

An **AI-powered maternal health chatbot** designed to support early risk detection and timely intervention during pregnancy.

### 🧠 How It Works
Health workers or patients enter basic pregnancy details such as:
- Maternal age  
- Trimester  
- Symptoms  
- Vital signs  
- Medical history  

The chatbot analyzes this data in **real time** to detect potential maternal health risks.

### 💬 Dual Chat System
- **Chat with AI**
  - Immediate guidance and risk alerts  
  - Basic maternal care recommendations  
  - Responses grounded using medical knowledge  

- **Chat with Doctor**
  - **24×7 access to a qualified doctor**
  - Triggered when high-risk conditions are detected
  - Enables timely expert consultation  

### 🌍 Impact
- Helps rural clinics act early  
- Reduces delays in risk identification  
- Improves safety for both mother and baby  

### ☁️ Deployment
Designed as a **SaaS platform**, deployable worldwide with minimal setup.

---

## 🧠 Key Technical Highlight: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

The chatbot uses **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** to ensure accurate and reliable healthcare responses:
- Retrieves relevant information from curated medical documents  
- Generates context-aware, document-grounded responses  
- Reduces hallucinations in healthcare-critical scenarios
